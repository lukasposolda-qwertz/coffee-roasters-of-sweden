    What is on the map

    Places in Sweden where coffee is roasted: roasteries, and cafés that roast their own beans. Some points are a roaster's café or shop rather than the roastery itself.

    • Roastery
    • Café that roasts on site
    Opening hours

    Hours come from OpenStreetMap and many roasteries have none listed. "Open now" uses your device's clock and does not know about public holidays, so check the roaster's own site before a long trip.

    Missing or wrong?

    The map shows what is in OpenStreetMap. Add a roastery or fix its details there, and it appears here after the next weekly update. Open OpenStreetMap →

    Data

    Every object tagged craft=coffee_roaster or microroasting=yes in Sweden. E-mail addresses and phone numbers are left out.

    Map tiles cannot load here, so the background is a plain outline of Sweden.
